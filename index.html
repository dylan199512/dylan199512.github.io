About Me

I help healthcare and payer organizations adopt AI safely, responsibly, and in alignment with emerging regulatory frameworks. My work focuses on governance, risk assessment, model oversight, and operational readiness for enterprise AI deployment. I also specialize in prior authorization and reimbursement in the AI healthcare space, helping organizations navigate the operational and regulatory complexities of integrating AI into clinical and administrative workflows.


Outside of AI governance, I am an independent registered songwriter with BMI and I write modern country music. I enjoy playing golf and spending time on the road between Dallas, Naples, Palm Springs, New York City, Toronto, and Nashville.
