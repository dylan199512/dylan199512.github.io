What I Do

I help health systems, payers, and policy teams govern AI systems used in reimbursement and prior authorization. My work focuses on clarity, oversight, and alignment with CMS rules so organizations can deploy AI with confidence and reduce regulatory and operational risk. I also understand how FDA’s evolving approach to AI and machine learning intersects with reimbursement, prior authorization, and healthcare operations.

Who I Help

  • Health systems
  • Payers
  • AI product teams
  • Compliance and risk teams
  • CMS adjacent policy groups

Problems I Solve

  • Understanding how CMS rules apply to AI models
  • Understanding how FDA’s AI and ML expectations intersect with reimbursement and prior authorization
  • Governing AI used in claims, coding, reimbursement, or prior authorization
  • Building explainability into model outputs
  • Reducing regulatory, operational, and reputational risk
  • Creating internal oversight frameworks
  • Communicating AI decisions clearly to clinicians, patients, and regulators

How I Work

I start by understanding your AI systems and where they intersect with reimbursement or prior authorization. I communicate everything in clear, direct language.
