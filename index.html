Tools & Projects

I build simple tools that help teams understand, test, or explain their AI systems. Here are a few examples:

ICD‑10 Explorer

A fast, lightweight tool for searching ICD‑10‑CM codes with clean filters, autocomplete, and structured detail pages. Built with Python, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, and Streamlit. It ingests the official ICD‑10‑CM source files, cleans them up, stores them in a structured database, and exposes everything through a clean, reactive UI.

Coding Validator — Synthetic Healthcare Claims Demo

A small demonstration of how claim validation can work using synthetic patient data, transparent rule logic, and FDA‑aligned governance thinking. All patient data is synthetic and does not represent real individuals.

Prior Authorization App

A simple Java application that shows how prior authorization logic can work in software. It includes a small rules engine, a basic UI, and an audit log. It gives a clear view of how UM decisions can be structured, explained, and recorded.

Congress AI — Healthcare & AI Legislation Chatbot

A retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG) system that lets you ask natural‑language questions about how members of Congress are engaging with healthcare and AI legislation across the 118th and 119th Congresses.

Services

What I Do

I help health systems, payers, and AI product teams make sure their AI systems follow the rules when they’re used for reimbursement, claims, coding, and prior authorization. I help you understand what your AI is doing, keep you out of trouble, and make sure everything lines up with CMS and FDA expectations.

Who I Help

  • Health systems
  • Payers and health plans
  • AI product teams
  • Compliance and risk teams
  • Groups working near CMS and health policy

Problems I Solve

  • Figuring out how CMS rules apply to your AI models.
  • Understanding what the FDA expects from AI and how that affects reimbursement.
  • Making sure AI used in claims, coding, or prior authorization behaves the way it should.
  • Explaining AI decisions in plain English.
  • Avoiding mistakes that could cost money or slow things down.
  • Setting up simple oversight so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Helping teams talk about AI clearly with clinicians, executives, and partners.

How I Work

I look at how your AI makes decisions, compare that to what CMS and the FDA expect, and point out anything that could cause problems. Then I help you fix it. Everything I build is meant to be used right away—no long reports, no confusing jargon.

What You Get

  • A clear framework for how to manage and oversee your AI.
  • Guidance that matches CMS and FDA expectations.
  • Simple ways to explain how your AI works.
  • A list of risks and practical ways to handle them.
  • Oversight steps so your team knows who’s responsible for what.
  • Clear language you can use with leadership, clinicians, and partners.
