About Me

Dylan Reid

I help healthcare and payer organizations adopt AI safely, responsibly, and in alignment with emerging regulatory frameworks. My work focuses on governance, risk assessment, model oversight, and operational readiness for enterprise AI deployment.

Outside of AI governance, I am an independent registered songwriter with BMI and I write modern country music. I enjoy playing golf and spending time on the road. I am often traveling between Dallas, Naples, Palm Springs, New York City, Toronto, and Nashville since those are the places I spend the most time.

What I Do

I specialize in building AI governance frameworks, evaluating model risk, and guiding organizations through compliance with FDA, HIPAA, GDPR, and emerging responsible AI standards for healthcare.

Areas of focus include:

  • Assess AI model risk and identify governance gaps
  • Advise healthcare organizations on safe and compliant AI deployment
  • Design human in the loop workflows for high risk use cases
  • Implement explainability standards for clinical and regulatory alignment
  • Build governance operating models that scale across the enterprise
